# Troubleshooting MarkShark

We're sorry if you're having trouble with MarkShark!  During development and testing the biggest issues tended to come from the alignment of scans to the template, or the ways that MarkShark would call a bubble correctly filled or not.

***Is it MarkShark or me?***
The issue *could* very well be an issue with the MarkShark software.  But before we get too far down a rabbit-hole:
- *were your scanned bubblesheets warped, very gray backgrounds, unevenly lit across the surface, poor contrast?*  If so, you may get better results by finding a better scanner or changing your settings to improve clarity or contrast, rather than tweaking a lot of software parameters to compensate for poor quality scans.
- *Is the problem with a few scans or all of them?*  Is there a pattern (like every other page is misaligned)?
